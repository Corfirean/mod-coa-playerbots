title: Custom races bot support and dedicated lore naming
---
Companion bots now respect the Custom Races bot switch and exclusion lists (CoACustomRaces.BotsEnable, AiPlayerbot.ExcludedBotRaces, CoaBots.CustomRaces.Enable). When enabled, bots spawn with lore-friendly names: unique races (Ogres, Sethrak, Naga, Vulpera, Pandaren, Tuskarr, Vrykul, Goblins, Jinyu, Gnolls, Saberon, Dracthyr, Furbolgs, Murlocs, Harpies) draw from dedicated lore name pools, while related sub-races reuse their canon racial naming conventions.
