title: Custom races bot support and dedicated lore naming
---
Companion bots now respect the Custom Races bot switch and exclusion lists. When enabled, bots spawn with lore names: unique races (Ogres, Sethrak, Naga, Vulpera, Pandaren, etc.) use dedicated name pools, while related sub-races reuse their canon racial naming conventions.
