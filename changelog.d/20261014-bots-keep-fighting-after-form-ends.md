title: Accursed and Domination bots keep fighting after their timed form ends
---
When a timed form with a long cooldown expired mid-fight, the bot stopped using abilities until the cooldown finished (about 26 seconds for Accursed and 44 seconds for Domination in a one-minute fight). They now keep using their other abilities and re-enter the form when it is ready.
