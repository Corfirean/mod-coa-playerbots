title: Bots no longer cast ally-only spells at enemies
---
Cultist and Primalist bots sometimes tried to use Void Shield, Eldritch Mending or Spirit Charge on the enemy and failed over and over. Tinker bots no longer use Mechsuit-only attacks outside the suit, and bots turn to face their target before casting.
