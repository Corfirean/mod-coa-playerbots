title: War, Infinite and Godblade bots cast their strongest attacks again
---
After the last round of bot changes, War bots cast Skulltaker less, Infinite bots cast Unmake less and Godblade bots stopped using Hammer of Twilight, so their damage dropped. Those attacks are back in the rotation, and Infinite bots no longer waste casts on a healing effect when nobody is hurt.
