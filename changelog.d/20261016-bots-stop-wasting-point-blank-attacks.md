title: Stormbringer bots no longer cast Discharge from range
---
Wind, Lightning and Maelstrom bots cast Discharge, which only hurts enemies within 5 yards, from across the arena and did no damage. They now use it only up close, or from range when they need the mana it returns. Wind bots deal about 70% more damage and Lightning bots about 50% more in training fights.
