audience: players
title: Bots cast many more of their real class abilities
---
About 130 abilities across 47 specializations were never picked by bots, even though human players use them constantly. Bots now cast them, ordered by damage per cast and cost, so paid spenders come before free fillers. In training fights bot damage rose by a median of about 20%, and Sanguine, Crusader, Voodoo, Soul, Heretic and Rime bots gained the most.
