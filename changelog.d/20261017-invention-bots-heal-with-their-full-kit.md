title: Invention Tinker bots use their whole healing kit
---
Invention bots now put Stim Augmentation on their gun, keep the Nanobot heal over time on the tank, fire Zap! on every cooldown, use Emergency Heal as their main heal, drop beacons when mana runs low and use My Greatest Invention! in a panic. Their healing in training fights rose by about two thirds and they survive much heavier damage.
