title: Maelstrom and Lightning bots cast Shock without pauses
---
Stormbringer bots waited four seconds between Shock casts and filled the gaps with a long weak channel. They now cast Shock whenever nothing stronger is ready, which raised Maelstrom damage in training fights by about half and Lightning damage by about a tenth. Accursed bots also spend less time idle after their form ends.
