title: Time and Heretic healer bots use more of their kit
---
Time bots now pick Aeon of Resilience, heal the whole party with Correct the Mistake, use Fortify Timeline and Temporal Anomaly, and no longer waste casts on Waves of Time, an enemy knockback. Their healing in training fights rose by about three quarters. Heretic bots stand in melee range, lay Black Blood with Malevolence, tether the tank with Abyssal Covenant and use Eldritch Eye, which raised their healing slightly.
