title: Weak DPS bots use the abilities their guides name
---
Several damage bots never cast key abilities: Stalking left its Brood Mark finishers unused, Maelstrom skipped Torrential Wrath, Headhunting kept its energy full without spending it, Hellfire ignored Seeking Flame and Pestilence of Apocalypse, Mechanics never built its Scrapmaw pet and Geomancy never cast Geode Barrage. They now use them. In training fights Stalking and Maelstrom deal about three and a half times the damage and Headhunting more than double, with smaller gains for Hellfire, Mechanics, Geomancy, Sentinel and Wildwalker.
