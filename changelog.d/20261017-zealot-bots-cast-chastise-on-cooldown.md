title: Zealot bots use Chastise far more often
---
Zealot bots held Chastise back to one cast every six seconds even though it hits several times harder than their other strikes. They now cast it whenever it is ready, which raised their damage in training fights by about a fifth. Several other Gladiator, Pyromancer, Reaper and Necromancer bots also rank their strongest attacks higher now.
