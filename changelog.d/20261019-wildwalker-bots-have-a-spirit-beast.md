title: Wildwalker bots now fight with a Spirit Beast
---
Wildwalker bots had no pet at all, so Savage Frenzy, Primal Shred and the pet half of Rylak's Bite never worked. A Wildwalker bot without a pet now gets a tamed wolf (or calls its stabled one back), set to defensive so it assists its owner, and uses Savage Frenzy, Primal Shred and Protective Roar. Artificer bots bank Echo Fragments with Paradox Cannon and Geomancy bots use Golem Form and Magma Fissure, which they never cast before.
