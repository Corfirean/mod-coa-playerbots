title: Bots actually fight, capture and score in battlegrounds
---
Bots in battlegrounds walked past enemies without attacking, healers never healed on the move, nobody could take a banner or pick up a flag, and Alterac Valley armies stood at their start. They now engage enemies in reach, capture Arathi Basin bases by casting on the banner and keep a defender on each base they hold, spread over the bases instead of all taking the nearest one, pick up and carry flags in Warsong Gulch and Eye of the Storm and score them, defend the flag room, march along the main road in Alterac Valley to meet the enemy army, and in arenas keep healers and casters at range.
