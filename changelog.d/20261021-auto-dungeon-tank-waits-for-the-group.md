title: The auto-dungeon tank waits for the group to catch up and recover
---
The auto-dungeon tank no longer walks on or pulls while a bot in the group is dead, far behind, low on mana or hurt. It stands until the bots have caught up and recovered (mana 80%, health 90%), and goes on after at most 150 seconds so one bot that never recovers cannot hold the run. Bots in an auto-dungeon now also sit and drink where they stand instead of waiting for the player leader to be close.
