title: Battleground bots now come from your level bracket
---
Bots that filled a battleground were taken from any online bot of the right faction without looking at its level, and queued into your bracket. A level 20 player could get level 30+ bots and even level 80 bots (shown as a skull) on both teams. Only bots whose own level fits the bracket are used now, so for a level 20 player everybody is level 29 or lower.
