title: Bots no longer run away from fights inside dungeons, raids and battlegrounds
---
The "this fight is lost, get out" retreat was written for the open world, but it also fired in dungeons and raids. A tank or healer that was low on health, or a group that had not damaged a boss for a while, made bots walk off mid-fight and drop aggro and healing. Bots now only retreat in the open world; in dungeons, raids, battlegrounds and arenas they fight on.
