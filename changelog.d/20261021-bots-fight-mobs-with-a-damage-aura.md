title: Bots fight mobs that carry a permanent damage aura instead of running from them
---
Bots treated the permanent damage aura of an ordinary mob (the irradiated gnomes in Gnomeregan, for example) as a ground hazard to step out of. Combat pulled them back in, they stepped out again, and the whole group stood about without fighting: the tank did not tank, the healer did not heal and melee bots kept backing away from the mob. Only a boss's own aura is avoided now. A Vizier (Venomancer healer) bot also no longer shifts into Spider Form, which belongs to the damage specializations.
