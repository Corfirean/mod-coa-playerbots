title: Damage dealers and healers run to the tank when a mob turns on them
---
When a mob in a dungeon or raid attacks a damage dealer or a healer, that bot now runs to the tank (stopping a cast if the tank is more than 10 yards away) so the tank can pick the mob up, instead of fighting on the spot or running into the next pack.
