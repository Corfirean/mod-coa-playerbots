title: Grouped bots in a battleground play the battleground instead of teleporting to you
---
A bot that was in your group and entered a battleground with you was teleported to the group leader again and again, because the group's "stay close to the leader" logic and the battleground AI both controlled it. The group logic now leaves a bot alone while it, or its leader, is in a battleground or arena.
