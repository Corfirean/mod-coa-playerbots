title: Healer bots walk up to an ally they cannot reach
---
A healer bot kept a standoff distance from the enemy even when the ally that needed healing was out of its range or out of its line of sight, so it stood back without healing and the group went down. The healer now first closes in on an ally it cannot reach (staying well inside its heal range), and only then takes up the standoff position against the enemy.
