title: Party bots follow a running leader instead of standing still
---
Bots in your party did not start following while you were running: their reaction delay was restarted every time it ran out, so they only moved once you stopped. After a fight they stood where they were while you ran on, which looked like bots stuck in place in dungeons. They now react within half a second and follow straight away; a bot that has already fallen far behind does not wait at all.
