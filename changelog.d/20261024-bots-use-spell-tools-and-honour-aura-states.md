title: Witch Doctor and Guardian bots use their totems and standards, and fewer casts are refused
---
Bots were never given the tool items some spells need in the bags, so Witch Doctors and Guardians could not cast their totems, wards and standards. They now receive them. Bots also check aura-state requirements the way the game does and wait out a cooldown the game still holds, which removes a number of refused casts (for example Rime's Glacial Impact and Runemaster's Zenith).
