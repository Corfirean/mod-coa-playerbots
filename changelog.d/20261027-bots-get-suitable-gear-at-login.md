area: bots
type: fixed
audience: players
title: Bots with unsuitable gear are refitted at login
---
A bot with empty armor slots, items above its level, or without the weapon type its spells need (a bow for a Sentinel) is put through the level-bracketed gear-up once per login and on level-up, through the throttled gear queue. Quest hand-ins and their failures are now logged with the reason.
