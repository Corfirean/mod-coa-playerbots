title: Bots with full bags make room to hand in finished quests
---
A finished quest cannot be handed in when the bags are full, and bots hoarded trade goods until they were: thousands of finished quests sat in the logs. Before a hand-in a bot now clears junk and, if needed, the least valuable stacks that no quest, tool or consumable needs. Places where bots keep dying are no longer chosen for quest work.
