title: Bots stay out of Outland and Northrend when those are not scaled to the level cap
---
With a level cap of 60 or lower and the content scaling for an expansion off, bots no longer spawn, quest, fly or travel on its map; bots found there are moved out. Blood Elf and Draenei starting areas stay open. CoaBots.RestrictExpansionZones sets this (2 automatic, 1 always, 0 never).
