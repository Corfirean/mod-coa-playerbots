title: A bot left far behind in a dungeon is called back to its group
---
When a group member has fallen far behind (for example after dying and coming back at the dungeon entrance), the tank now calls it to the group after 45 seconds instead of waiting two and a half minutes every few minutes. A group that is held in combat by creatures it cannot reach also breaks the fight off after a while.
