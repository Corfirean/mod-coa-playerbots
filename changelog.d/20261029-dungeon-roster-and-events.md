title: Bot dungeon groups follow a route through each dungeon, including wings and scripted events
---
Bots running a dungeon on their own now work through a per-dungeon list of bosses and objectives instead of just walking to the nearest boss. Dungeons with several wings are handled one wing at a time, forbidden zones and route hints keep groups on walkable paths, and the scripted parts of many Classic, Burning Crusade and Wrath dungeons (levers, gossip, escorts, wave defences, the Violet Hold siege, the Culling of Stratholme waves) are performed by the group's tank.
