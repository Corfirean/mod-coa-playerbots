title: Bots in dungeons and raids hit harder, heal more, take less damage and hold aggro better
---
Bots now get a configurable help in dungeons and raids (CoaBots.Scaling.* in mod_coa_playerbots.conf): by default they deal 60% more damage, heal 40% more, take 30% less damage from creatures and bot tanks generate 50% more threat. Pets and totems of a bot count as the bot. The open world and battlegrounds are not changed. The creatures themselves are not weakened, so groups that include a person feel the same as before.
