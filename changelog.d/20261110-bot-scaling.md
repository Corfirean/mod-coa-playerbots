title: Optional extra strength for bots in dungeons and raids
---
Server owners can give bots extra damage and healing, less damage taken and more tank threat (CoaBots.Scaling.*),
separately for dungeons, raids, battlegrounds and the open world. Every multiplier starts at 1, so nothing changes
until you raise them in the Manager or the config. Reloadable with .reload config.
