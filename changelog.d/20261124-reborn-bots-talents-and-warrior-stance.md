title: Reborn bots spend talent points, keep pets and buffs up, and fight with more abilities
---
Warcraft Reborn bots never picked talents; from level 10 they now spend their points on a build for their spec, and a hunter's pet spends its own. Their combat profiles use more of what a class has (cooldowns, interrupts, stuns, area attacks, damage over time), and bots take specs by the realm's tank and healer shares instead of by chance. Hunters get a wolf and ammunition, warlocks a demon, rogues poisons, shamans weapon imbues and totems, paladins a seal and blessings, mages and warlocks an armor, and druids, priests and mages buff their group. Abilities a Reborn trainer teaches that bots missed are learned, and gear is scored by each spec's stat.
