# Overnight soak, 2026-10-27 23:44 - 2026-10-28 04:50

Branch `integration/encounters-and-fixes` (combat-intelligence work merged with `feat/dynamic-bot-encounters`), sandbox world
with up to 857 bots, workers for dungeons, battlegrounds, the dungeon finder and forced encounters, a check every 40 minutes
(`.agents/soak/checks/`, not in git). The world was rebuilt and restarted nine times for the fixes below, so the dungeon rounds
that overlapped a restart ended in ERROR; the full uninterrupted Classic round (round 16) is the best dungeon sample.

## Fixed during the run

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Bots called by the dungeon finder did not join the player | A filler bot queues with its saved dungeon-finder mode. 1927 of ~2000 bots hold the old default (matchmaking), which content scaling turns into "current party": each filler formed a one-player proposal, accepted it and went into a dungeon alone while the player waited. | `BotLfgFill` forces a five-player standard-role queue for the bots it queues (GlobalScript `OnResolveLfgQueuePolicy`). |
| Some fillers still stayed behind | LFGMgr teleports the group once and refuses a falling player; bots keep a "falling" flag for good (no client reports the landing), or are mounted. | `RetryLfgTeleport`: every 4 s for the first minute, dismounts, clears the stale falling flag, teleports again; stops once the bot has arrived. |
| World thread stalls of 0.5-0.7 s every five seconds at 850 bots | `BotMgr::IsBotAccountId` ran a synchronous login-database query (`AccountMgr::GetName`) on every call; the encounter director calls it twice per online player per evaluation. | The answer is cached. Evaluation cost 467 ms -> 0.3 ms at similar population; tick mean at 857 bots was 40 ms before. |
| 2500 STUCK-DETECTOR lines per 40 min in the open world | Bots attacked full-health mobs they could not reach (the mob never fought back) and stayed for good. | The target is ignored for 90 s (grind, loot). Down to ~40. |
| Dungeon-team bots left for the auction house | Bot economy trips ignored groups, battlegrounds and dungeons. | Trips are skipped and cancelled for those bots. |
| Battleground bots flew to a point "in a row" | Every bot of a team got the same target point. | Long walks end at a per-bot offset (up to 9 yd, angle and radius from the guid). Measured spread in a battleground: 126-226 yd mean distance from the centroid. |

## Findings, not fixed

- Auto-dungeon: 94% of scout runs end in the 10-minute timeout without a boss (by design of the scout), the group is in combat ~55% of
  the time and waits for a caster's mana the rest; Details in `.agents/soak/dungeons/summary.log`. Repeating STUCK
  runs (gnomer, scholo, sv, ramparts, up, vh, mara, lbrs) end with "the navigation mesh has no path to this point": the goal lies on another
  level (goal z 50 below the group), so it is a navmesh or door problem, not the wait logic.
- Ghost corpse run: ~4000 "CorpseRun: movement refused: no mesh path" lines per 40 minutes; the 20 s fallback revives the bot where it stands.
- Warsong Gulch score stays 0:0 in the available time; Arathi Basin scores (550:490 seen) and bases are captured. Eye of the Storm and Alterac Valley
  do not open for bots at the cap-60 bracket.
- Encounters (forced and natural): ~108 ended in 3 hours, 105 with `cleanup=PASS`; types that need a real player (assassin, watcher, bounty, nemesis,
  mystery) cannot be completed by bots alone. Rare hunters end by timeout or participant death most often.
- Bot economy: 76 trips, 15 auctions listed, 147 vendor sales and 17 gold earned in the last 45 minutes of one generation; trips are long (up to 17 000 yd).
- Gear refit logs many false positives ("the weapon type the spells need is not equipped" for classes that have no such weapon).
- One world process vanished at 01:44 without a crash dump.
